## Proposed conceptual distinctions to test

- **Praxis**: a repeatable way of becoming equipped, acting, and learning.
- **Architecture epic**: why a capability domain matters, its boundaries, and
  how concrete work fits. Not a permanently “in progress” pseudo-module.
- **Happening**: a bounded or ongoing cross-session episode of work with an
  originating pressure, related events, and an observable account of change.
  It might use an epic, but chronology and blocking dependencies are not the
  same relationship.
- **Capability**: an observable thing a system can do. A CLI flag such as
  `--server` can be a consequential capability, but merely seeing a flag name
  does not prove it reaches the intended downstream transport.
- **Changelog projection**: a derived account of owner-native work events, not
  a parallel manually maintained lifecycle or summaries that trigger endless
  summaries of themselves.

These are hypotheses for investigation, not a newly accepted ontology.
