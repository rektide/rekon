## Chase the future

**Build toward what current upstream makes possible, not only what an older
installation happens to expose. Choose a useful next step that opens more of
the possibility space.**

We want ambition and concrete movement together. A somewhat bolder implementation
that gives us a reusable probe, a new observation, or a working consumer may be
worth more than the smallest diff. When the needed interface is known and the
work is authorized, build that slice now. Uncertainty about the eventual
destination is not a reason to defer every implementable part.

The discipline is to keep the claims honest: source support, a working client,
successful deployment, hardware behavior, and a useful end-to-end result are
different achievements. Neither caution nor ambition should erase those
differences.

Keep **both the course we are taking and other courses of enduring interest**.
An alternate destination is not necessarily rejected, a fallback, or a future
milestone. Record why it matters, what supports it, the cost of exploring or
preserving it, and what would bring it back into consideration. That map can
remain usefully ambiguous while the next action has a clear boundary.

## Related practice: coverage

[Coverage](/praxis/coverage/README.md) collects the threads in a conversation or
pass, says what happened to each, and names important topics left out without
converting all of them into commitments.

## Related practice: candidates

[Candidates](/praxis/candidates/README.md) gives meaningfully different
possibilities a fair hearing before choosing a course. The human explicitly
wants more than a single predetermined answer: develop candidates, compare
their strengths and costs, and retain worthwhile alternatives. Regathering
and focusing after expansion are part of the practice, not a rejection of it.
This first account is draft guidance; it does not require a multi-agent wave
for every choice.

## Related practice: parallax

Before selecting or integrating a design, [parallax](/praxis/parallax/README.md)
develops deliberately different positions on the same system. Independent
same-question research and complementary design axes expose different kinds of
evidence; the parent compares and synthesizes after the relevant gates rather
than predetermining the architecture or averaging the outputs.

## Related practice: flights

[Flights](/praxis/flights/README.md) coordinate agent promises across explicit
spawn and join points. Their optional full-cycle example carries independent
research through syntheses and designs without making that sequence a universal
pipeline; gates, partial results, and human authority remain explicit.
The [Kinefolk firmware flight](/praxis/flights/kinefolk-firmware0.gpt6a.md)
records a completed research launch pattern and observed learning, without
claiming demonstrated runtime behavior or general practice success.

## Related practice: after the build

[Post-build possibility gathering](/praxis/post-build/README.md) starts from a
completed vertical and asks which shared foundations and broader offerings are
now worth pursuing. Its separate motivating commission is the
[colorlu / luf-settings / luffect assessment](/praxis/post-build/colorlu/possibilities0.gpt6a.md):
turn the first application's special knowledge into stronger shared contracts,
and preserve the newly reachable paths rather than merely add another module.
This complements Chase the future; it does not inherit the wireless case's
product work anchor or turn its proposals into authorized implementation.

## Related practice: fronts and horizons

[Fronts and horizons](/praxis/fronts/README.md) compares strategic directions
with tactical next moves at different payoff horizons: put the foundation
to use, compose adjacent capabilities, or change the offering. It asks what
each direction buys and separates a direct practical recommendation from
preserved options. This equips the choice at a crossroads; bookmarking makes
that junction recoverable. Horizons are not delivery dates, and a front map
is not an automatically authorized roadmap. It is also the hub of the fronts
thread: its [addendum](/praxis/fronts/README.md#fronts-thread) groups the
nearby map/orientation practices — chase-future, post-build, candidates,
high/low assessment, digest's intent fronts, the lineage research-fronts
origin, and bookmarking's crossroads — around it with ⇄ maintained links.

## Related tactic: README-driven development

[README-driven development](/praxis/readme-driven/README.md) starts with the
experience we want the project to offer. A future-forward README makes that
destination readable, distinguishes it from what works today, and equips a
human and collaborating agents to steer the next coherent slice against real
upstream possibilities. Its motivating use is the iroh-aware direction inquiry;
it is draft guidance, not a new implementation mandate or installed skill.

## Related practice: workflow diagrams

[Workflow diagrams](/praxis/workflow-diagrams/README.md) make actions, evidence,
feedback, release conditions, and preserved options visible as named Mermaid
maps. The PTP/Sol rotation case shows why a scoped negative capability result
can inform tooling work without blocking independent product work. This applies
the existing naming and evidence-gate policies; it creates no second tracker.

## Related practice: ticket pointers

[Beads work tracking](/praxis/beads/README.md) owns the concrete operating
practice: explicit readable IDs, epic-child namespaces, dependency direction,
and the search/update/close loop. Its selected GLOBAL makes these rules available
before an agent creates tickets. It complements the pointer practice below.

[Ticket pointers](/praxis/ticket-pointers/ticket-pointers0.glm53fx.md) keep each
work ticket a curated pointer at the two to four documents that currently best
explain its work, moved to the tip as design waves, roadmaps, and check-ins
emerge. A ticket is a pointer to the tip, not a snapshot; pointer rot is the
named failure mode, and an explorer for ticket link history is a filed desire
(`rekon-ticket-link-explorer`), not an installed tool. This uses the existing
beads append-notes mechanics and the balanced ticket coordination division of
labor; it creates no second tracker.

## Related practice: consumer accounts

[Consumer accounts](/praxis/consumer-accounts/README.md) give consuming projects
a voice in the libraries they use: current adoption, case studies, friction and
improvement hopes, linked to native consumer records. Recommend this library-side
documentation pattern for libraries we use under `~/src`; Kubeless's
`doc/consumer/` supplies the concrete precedent.

## Related practice: bookmarking

[Bookmarking](/praxis/bookmarking/README.md) marks the session material worth
returning to — decision-dense junctions, explicit summaries, review and design
gates — as durable, owner-native bookmarks in cotail's landed engine, with a
seed tag glossary (`crossroads`, `summary`, `review`, `design-gate`) whose
canonical meanings stay cotail-side (`doc/bookmarks/tag-glossary.md`, ticket
`cotail-bookmark-praxis`). One naming caution for readers of this tree:
"praxis" also names [doc/praxis.glm.md](file:///home/rektide/src/doc/praxis.glm.md),
the xstate-as-execution-model practice guide — a **domain technique guide** in
the doc repo, not a Rekon action posture. Rekon's `praxis/` holds the latter
sense; the two share a word, not a home.

## Make the practice easier to do

The human's direction is that this too is praxis **we should engine**: give the
posture useful mechanisms, not just a slogan. Start with the actual action—an
executable probe, reproducible fixture, source-revision capture, or comparison
that makes the next question cheaper to answer. Keep those mechanisms with their
product or domain owner. Return the learning about how to choose and commission
them here.

A reusable course form is supplied now. Automated option reminders, upstream
delta discovery, or a skill exposure remain possibilities to evaluate through
use, not an authorized platform build. This directory does not install a skill,
register a CLI command, or change ambient AGENTS guidance.
