# Coverage — account for threads and omissions

When the human asks **“coverage?”**, gather the threads in the relevant prompt or
conversation, re-present how each was addressed, remains pending, is deferred or
blocked, and name important adjacent topics not dealt with in this pass. Be clear
about the examined scope and uncertainty; distinguish suggestions from decisions
and results. Do not turn every thread into a ticket or a commitment. Deeper practice:
[coverage](/praxis/coverage/README.md).
