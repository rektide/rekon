# Coverage: what did we pick up, and what did we leave out?

The local [GLOBAL contribution](/praxis/coverage/GLOBAL.md) proposes an ambient
cue. It is not selected by the current root manifest; its existence alone does not
mean it is loaded into shared context.

When a human offers several ideas, requests, corrections or questions, they can
ask **“coverage?”** to request a compact account of what happened to the threads.
This is a conversation and handoff aid, not a requirement to work on everything.

## Make the pass

1. Gather the actual threads in the relevant prompt or agreed conversation scope;
   preserve the human's meaning and note ambiguous interpretations. Include
   questions, decisions and dependencies, not just executable tasks.
2. Re-present each thread with its present disposition: addressed and how,
   in progress, proposed, deferred/parked, blocked, or not yet addressed. Link
   an artifact or evidence when helpful. Separate an answer, an experiment and
   an implemented capability; do not turn a suggestion into acceptance.
3. Name important adjacent topics **not being dealt with in this pass**, including
   gaps a reader might reasonably think were covered. Distinguish explicit
   exclusions from omissions or uncertain coverage. Say what sources and scope
   were examined; don't imply that the list is exhaustive if it isn't.
4. Highlight unresolved choices, dependencies and next possible moves in
   proportion to the user's purpose. Ask when a consequential ambiguity needs
   their decision rather than silently expanding scope.

A short table or a few grouped bullets are enough. If the user asks during active
work, distinguish the results already obtained from work that is still pending;
revisit the pass when those results arrive. The conversation, not a new ticket,
can be the entire output. Create or update durable records or work items only
when requested or independently authorized.

## Relation to fronts

[Fronts and horizons](/praxis/fronts/README.md) compares worthwhile directions
and practical moves. Coverage asks whether the present conversation or pass has
accounted for its threads and omissions. A coverage pass can inform a fronts
discussion without selecting a course, proving completeness, or making every
named possibility a commitment.
