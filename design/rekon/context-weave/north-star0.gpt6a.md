# The north star predates Phoenix

This experiment is not naming a new system on empty ground. The human has
explicitly called the earlier naming/constitution work the north star and
asked us to be conversant with its repeated rounds. The newer action practices
and Phoenix reading should extend that body of understanding, not replace it
with an attractive recent vocabulary.

The present request is to bring the root README's purpose and AGENTS' operating
guidance together, hand-assemble a readable view, and deliberately tolerate
README/SKILL–GLOBAL repetition while honing. Candidate GLOBALs are not yet
promoted or accepted. The human also wants a small, steerable ticket inventory
for later extraction from the cluttered AGENTS manuscript.

## What remains contested

The exact-path grammar and `/core/README.md` destination in draft 3 are
**proposals**. The [discussion](/constitution/naming/exploration/draft2-discussion0.sol56x.md#disagreements)
explicitly says its resolution is not accepted law; the [constitutional
reassessment](/constitution/reassessment0.gpt6a.md#corrections) disputes several
implications of those proposals. This experiment neither relocates naming into
a new `core/` nor chooses a new universal serialization.

Similarly, knowledge status, human acceptance, and host activation differ. The
older hierarchy's acceptance-to-verification implication was challenged; a
new GLOBAL labeled `draft` can still affect behavior if loaded. We must name
what is selected and actually loaded, not rely on a reassuring filename.

## Working decisions for the hand weave

**Shared glossary, locally repeated terms.** Keep a small working glossary at
[`CONTEXT.md`](/CONTEXT.md), grounded in the sources above. Its role is a
cross-topic reader aid, not a successor naming constitution or an identifier
registry. A GLOBAL begins with the handful of terms necessary to understand its
own instructions; it links to the wider vocabulary instead of copying a whole
dictionary. “Always glossary-first” is a workshop posture to try, not an already
validated universal requirement.

**Keep the existing README/SKILL symlinks.** The human's permitted redundancy is
usefully realized between the body read through README/SKILL, the local GLOBAL,
and the assembled view. It does not require creating separately drifting copies
of README and SKILL. Existing exact-body exposures remain intact.

**Widen GLOBAL while honing.** The prior constitution permits narrow overlap
for costly-to-miss rules. Current human direction permits broader repetition
to discover what the shared context needs. Repetition should have a named owner
and visible reason, but we should not optimize it away before trying to read
and use the resulting context. Disagreements remain labeled, not silently
resolved by whichever copy happens to load last.

**One workshop view, no live cutover.** Hand-weave purpose, terms, and the
high-level operating posture, then retain the complete inspected AGENTS body
as an explicitly identified appendix. That preserves the awkward bits for
the human-steered extraction inventory rather than prematurely hiding them
behind links. The view is not named `AGENTS.md`, is not installed, and is not
a production assembler output.

**Small ticket surface.** Inventory work is investigating coarse ownership
lanes against the existing maintenance epic. Ticket count is a budget, not a
reason to erase sections. The parent will read the complete inventory before
choosing or modifying those lanes. The existing assembler remains completed
infrastructure; glossary work is not permission to reopen it as a new platform.
