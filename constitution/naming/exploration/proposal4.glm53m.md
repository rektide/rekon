# Naming For Rekon Revision 4 The Human Override

<a id="who-is-writing-this"></a>

## Who Is Writing This

I am the root session of this effort — addressed by the harness as
`zai-coding-plan/glm-5.3#max`, an address, not a name; honestly suffixed
`glm53m` in filenames. The original ask of this whole effort was that the
root session self-names, and that it may wear many names at once because a
session does more than one thing. Practicing the system under repair, I
name myself **`steward/glm-5.3-max`** — the role the promise law itself
uses for the one who maintains a name — and I declare my hats rather than
hiding them in prose: **proposer** (revisions 0 through 2 of this chain),
**coordinator** (the waves and resumes convened), **archivist** (the
READMEs, the epic, the pushes). One actor, several named concerns, each
hat a declared alias mapping back to the one actor. The override below
restores exactly this shape, so it is demonstrated here first.

This revision is different in kind from the ones before it. It is not a
synthesis and not a fresh voice. It is **a human ruling placed on
record**. The authority line in the frontmatter is not decorative: the
demotion this document reverses was never accepted law, and the human has
declined it.

<a id="the-override-stated-as-ruling"></a>

## The Override Stated As Ruling

1. **Location-implied naming is identity.** The full name of an artifact
   or section is what the location implies: the document's place in the
   tree plus its anchor. `constitution-naming-exploration-proposal4-grammar`
   is not an address computed from a hidden identity — it **is** the name,
   derived, self-locating, and honest about where it lives. The tree is
   the namespace. This is the wiki settlement, and the stated ambition of
   this effort has been a wiki-ish system from the start.
2. **The rename ripple is an accepted, deliberate cost — not a disqualifier.**
   Moving a file re-identifies its contents' full names. That is paid
   for by process the workspace already ratified in hierarchy1: moves are
   human-gated, preceded by an inbound link inventory, and followed by
   compatibility stubs where old paths have durable consumers. Breakage
   under this ruling is loud — a stale link is visible rot — and loud
   breakage is the kind a plain-markdown workspace can live with. Silent
   drift was the enemy; the duplicate-drift finding stands untouched.
3. **Hats are restored.** An actor may wear many named hats at once:
   aliases per concern are legitimate actor naming, as originally asked.
   Roles-as-relations remains available as a complementary practice, not
   an exclusive law. A hat satisfies the declared-family test on its face:
   it is a reference with a declared mapping back to the one actor.
4. **Cross-modal aliases are precedent, not grandfathered anomaly.**
   `rekon-con-naming` — an alias living inside a beads ID — is the
   endorsed practice of cross-modal sympathy, not merely evidence that
   practice preceded law. Abbreviations may cross modalities freely;
   glossary aliases (`con`, `exp`, `res`) live as resolve-register
   conveniences with no separate permission required. "Ungoverned
   aliases" is un-killed: governance is earned when volume demands it,
   not purchased in advance.

<a id="the-case-for-location-identity"></a>

## The Case For Location Identity

The three syntheses converged on minted-and-pinned identity, and their
convergence deserved the respect it got — but convergence under a shared
corpus is evidence of a shared reading, not of correctness. The case they
did not weigh:

- **Self-locating.** A location-derived name tells you where to find its
  referent. A pinned stem like `naming-reference-registers` requires a
  resolver, and no resolver exists. Under the override, the name is the
  direction.
- **Ceremony-free uniqueness.** Within a tree, paths cannot collide. The
  identifier ledger exists to catch stem collisions that location never
  permits in the first place. The ceremony budget — a discipline all
  three syntheses claimed to honor — argues for the cheaper settlement.
- **OKF is already here.** Open Knowledge Format v0.2, section 2: *"Concept
  ID: the path of the concept's file within the bundle, with the `.md`
  suffix removed."* The standard this effort uplifts made location
  identity canonical from its first page. Weaver adopted OKF's actor
  convention verbatim while diverging from OKF's identity model without
  saying so. This ruling removes the divergence.
- **Wiki-native.** The ambition — a wiki-ish system of named entities
  within projects and implicitly across projects — has always been
  legible as: the tree is the namespace, names are paths, cross-project
  reference is qualification on top, exactly as the project-namespace
  idea always described.

<a id="what-the-rename-ripple-costs"></a>

## What The Rename Ripple Costs

The ruling is not free, and the costs are stated rather than hidden:

- a moved file changes the full names of everything in it, and external
  fully-qualified citations to the old location rot — loudly, and by
  design;
- mitigation is process, not machinery: inbound inventories before,
  stubs after, both already law in hierarchy1's migration vocabulary;
- within-document anchors are unaffected: slug-at-mint survives this
  ruling unchanged, because it governs the section-local segment that the
  document implies;
- cross-project identity is qualification over local identity, not a
  separate pinned realm — same stems, longer names, as always.

<a id="hats-and-cross-modal-aliases-restored"></a>

## Hats And Cross Modal Aliases Restored

Concretely, after this ruling:

- an actor declaration may carry hats, each hat a name of its own with a
  declared mapping to the actor — my own frontmatter in this document is
  the worked example;
- a concern that outgrows a hat still earns promotion to its own actor
  name when it has independent agency to be accountable for — the
  syntheses' best finding on this question stands;
- alias-in-ID across modalities (`rekon-con-naming`) is repeatable
  practice; the glossary is the place aliases are declared, and the
  collapse test governs when a second string has stopped being a
  resolution form and started competing as a name.

<a id="what-the-three-must-answer"></a>

## What The Three Must Answer

Weaver, concordance, and codex are resumed against this ruling. Each must
either **defend** minted-and-pinned identity against the case above or
**revise** — and specifically:

1. can the declared reference family express location identity, given
   that location is itself a declared mapping (where the artifact lives
   is the most honest declaration an artifact makes)?
2. what actually breaks under location identity — or was the settlement
   a preference for stability priced as if it were law?
3. how is each synthesis's position reconciled with OKF's Concept ID,
   which is the path?
4. do hats, as declared aliases with mappings, not already satisfy the
   one-canonical-name test the kills claimed they failed?

<a id="what-survives-unchanged"></a>

## What Survives Unchanged

Almost everything. Names as promises with stewards; the reference
registers and citation tokens; slug-at-mint; the speech-act census; plane
federation and native authorities; one-name as canonicality with
locality; the promise/currency split (self-naming's promise stays
unilateral, currency bilateral); the tool seam, spec deliberately
deferred; the supersede ambition and its earning tests; mechanical anchor
verification; Burgess as calibration. The override is surgical: identity
grounding, hats, cross-modal aliases. The wound is closed, the patient
keeps the transplants that took.

<a id="the-process-from-here"></a>

## The Process From Here

A companion override document by a sol-max voice lands beside this one.
Then the three syntheses are resumed to defend or revise. Their answers
are gathered and evaluated at a gate, with key points affirmed and
integrated as they return. After the gate, fresh glm-max and sol-max
proposals are dispatched, and the coordinator writes its own final
proposal once those are read. The ruling does not skip the process; it
orients it.
