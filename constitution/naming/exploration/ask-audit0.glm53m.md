## The Receipts

The casualty fates above, in the syntheses' own words:

- **weaver** (`proposal3.glm53m.md`): killed "abbreviations in canonical
  stems (`rekon-con-naming` grandfathered as finding, not precedent)";
  "hats-as-aliases (revised to roles-on-edges — the identifier kept, the
  reading superseded)"; "proposal2's implied-names identity equation (kept
  as resolution convention; identity is minted and pinned)".
- **concordance** (`proposal3.sol56x.md`): killed "path-defined identity,
  one byte string across all media, ungoverned aliases, hats as actor
  names, unilateral global self-naming, and Beads-as-universal-store."
- **codex** (`proposal3b.glm53m.md`): killed "unmapped competing semantic
  names, path-defined identity, abbreviations in canonical stems,
  hats-as-aliases, unilateral global self-naming and coordinator
  christening alike."

One divergence of note inside the receipts: concordance's "one byte
string across all media" was killed while cross-modal contortion — the
endorsed norm — was never byte-identity. The ruling restores alias
crossings freely; byte-identity across hostile media remains correctly
dead. Contortion is not duplication.
