supporting evidence; inclusion does not confer acceptance. Status checked against
the local documents and live Beads records on **2026-09-04**.

## Keeping This Map Useful

- Link the current account and significant unresolved alternatives, not every
  artifact in a long wave. Leave full listings with their topic owners.
- Say whether a target is maintained guidance, a recorded decision, a proposal,
  implementation evidence, or history. Do not infer acceptance from filename,
  date, symlink, model agreement, or ticket closure alone.
- After a significant wave, decision, promotion, or move, check this index and
  the affected topic landing page together. Update explanations and validate
  paths/fragments; preserve exact historical citations.
- Keep ticket lifecycle in Beads. The [work map](/constitution/work-map0.gpt6a.md)
  is a dated orientation, not a second work tracker.
