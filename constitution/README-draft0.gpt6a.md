<a id="purpose"></a>
# Rekon: Carry Understanding Forward

Rekon helps people and agents make technical work cumulative. We work on
systems, but also on the understanding through which those systems are changed:
questions, concepts, evidence, decisions, methods, tools, and relationships.
Each effort should leave the next person better able to understand the situation
and choose a useful action.

Our shared medium lets us think together across sessions and projects. A
question can become an inquiry; an inquiry can change our terms; a proposal can
be challenged; a decision can acquire a practical method; an attempt can reveal
that the method needs to change. None of these transitions is guaranteed by
producing a file or closing a ticket. They depend on the relationships we
preserve and the judgments we make visible.

This constitution holds the shared commitments. Domain homes own the fuller
methods and mechanisms. A good constitutional account helps us recognize what
we are doing and where to continue without requiring every task to traverse the
entire system.

<a id="markdown"></a>
## Think And Talk In Markdown

Markdown is a working surface for thought and exchange. We write while an idea
is taking shape: its situation, grounds, alternatives, questions, and proposed
consequences. A document can be an invitation to respond, not only a report of
finished work.

When understanding must survive a conversation, give it a durable place where
another person or agent can examine and answer it. Link the thought being
answered. Say what changed, what prompted the change, and what remains open.
Keep enough context for a later reader to resume the inquiry rather than merely
receive its conclusion.

This is selective externalization of useful reasoning, not a demand to retain
every utterance or disclose every intermediate mental step. Questions, reasons,
evidence, and consequential changes deserve a public working record in the
appropriate scope. Disposable exploration can stay disposable until others
need to rely on it.

**Making a thought referable does not make it settled.** Drafts, disagreements,
and unanswered questions are legitimate durable knowledge.

<a id="checkpoints"></a>
## Preserve Coherent Stages Of Thought

Commit work as coherent stages become available. Research, prompts, proposals,
and guidance have stages just as implementation does. A clear commit explains
the change and its reason; it makes the development of understanding inspectable.

Before a substantial review-driven revision, preserve the current owned state.
Then record the correction as a distinct change with its intended destination.
Materially different arguments and independent voices ordinarily receive new
artifacts; an author's ordinary refinements can remain changes to their own
work. Preserve enough lineage to tell which happened.

A checkpoint preserves a state. Acceptance selects a direction or result.
Verification checks a stated property. These are different acts, even when they
happen near one another. Frequent commits should make the discussion clearer,
not create a performance of progress through meaningless fragments.

<a id="relations"></a>
## Give Understanding Recoverable Names And Relationships

We need to recognize what we are discussing across documents, specifications,
agents, tickets, tools, and sessions. Wiki-native knowledge uses its location
and section as part of its name. Scoped aliases can make that knowledge easier
to discuss across different kinds of records.

This commitment has several parts:

- **Referability:** a reader can identify the particular thought or artifact.
- **Ownership:** a native owner maintains the relevant name, state, or meaning.
- **Continuity:** later changes explain what they preserve, revise, or replace.
- **Attribution:** authorship, model, execution, and responsibility remain
  distinguishable where those differences matter.
- **Relationship:** a link explains support, dependence, contrast, succession,
  or another meaningful connection rather than merely suggesting proximity.

Similar words do not establish identical referents. A work item and the document
it commissions may share a convenient abbreviation while remaining different
things. An actor's hat is a scoped way of addressing that actor, not necessarily
a new actor. A channel selecting the current artifact is distinct from each
artifact it has selected.

Cite maintained homes for present navigation. Bind exact evidence to an
immutable revision when the claim depends on the specific version. A move of a
durable source changes its knowledge names: agree the change and account for
affected references, including the compatibility being promised. The detailed
grammar and migration machinery belong with naming's own work.

<a id="grounds"></a>
## Keep Claims, Grounds, And Authority Distinguishable

A useful account makes clear what was observed, what follows from it, what is
inferred, and what someone proposes or requires. Evidence is a source's role in
supporting or challenging a claim under particular conditions. A citation is a
route to those grounds, not a transferable certificate of truth.

Authority is scoped. The person selecting a direction, the owner of a work
record, the maintainer of a source, and the checker of a result answer different
questions. A newer model synthesis cannot silently revoke an earlier human
decision. A human's policy acceptance does not automatically verify every
factual assertion used to recommend it.

State the extent of a check and its limits. A local test, successful assembly,
empty search, or responsive service proves a particular thing. Do not let its
convenient label imply a larger guarantee. When the grounds change or no longer
support the claim, make the uncertainty visible and reopen the relevant judgment.

<a id="inquiry"></a>
## Let Difference Improve The Inquiry

Independent work earns its cost when different approaches or coverage could
change our understanding. Preserve independence while it is part of the
evidence; then provide a deliberate occasion for comparison and response.

Synthesis is an argued integration. It identifies common ground, disagreements,
distinctive contributions, and reasons to select or reject claims. Agreement
between models is evidence about their readings, not a vote that settles truth
or authority. A minority insight may expose the most consequential failure.

Strong research machinery—dependency-aware dispatch, sealed inputs, manifests,
adversarial passes—belongs where it answers a real coordination or evidence
problem. The shared obligation is honest comparison and recoverable lineage;
not every task needs a wave.

<a id="homes"></a>
## Maintain A Present Account Without Erasing The Conversation

Each durable concern should have a recognizable home that explains what to
understand or use now, where the grounds live, and which questions remain open.
A reader should not have to reconstruct every historical wave to find the
present account.

Promotion reconciles understanding into that home. It does not retroactively
make the source artifacts say what we now believe. Current prose can be edited
coherently; independent and historical arguments retain their identities and
lineage. History should remain available without making the current account a
chronological pile of corrections.

Organize by domains that can own their meaning. The directory locates knowledge;
it does not establish maturity, acceptance, or truth. Respect upstream-native
organization and preserve a coherent place for workspace-specific inquiry.
Adopting better knowledge contracts need not require moving the corpus.

<a id="attention"></a>
## Make Knowledge Available When It Can Help

Knowledge needs a path into action. A skill gives a capability a recognizable
activation boundary, relevant method, and completion contract. A reference
supplies depth; an ambient contribution supplies what must be available before
someone recognizes a need to load more.

Keep one owner of meaning across these exposures. Repeated wording can serve
different attention needs when its owner and maintenance relationship are clear.
Judge delivery by whether people and agents reach useful understanding and
complete the intended work—not only by file size or a smaller context count.

Changing source ownership and changing when guidance is seen are independently
significant changes. Preserve existing obligations while reorganizing their
sources; evaluate activation changes deliberately. Generated views and assembled
context must remain traceable to their inputs, with visible drift and failures.

<a id="direction"></a>
## Keep Direction Revisable And Work Concrete

Exploration opens and maps possibilities. Navigation chooses a course from a
known position, with observable signs of arrival and reasons to stop or reroute.
Neither substitutes for the native work graph's commitments and dependencies.

Keep desired and rejected properties visible without assuming that an omitted
preference means opposition. Preserve worthwhile alternate courses; a useful
future need not already be an authorized implementation. Let evidence and human
direction change the course explicitly.

Use tickets where ownership, lifecycle, dependencies, or acceptance need to
survive the immediate exchange. Documents carry explanations and grounds;
tickets carry the undertaking. Connect them where useful without demanding
one-to-one parity or manufacturing a backlog from every named idea.

<a id="learning"></a>
## Return Experience To The Practice

An action should leave evidence of what it attempted, what occurred, and what
that result permits us to conclude. When we are trying a practice, compare the
help expected with the help actually obtained. Completion of its prescribed
artifact is not sufficient evidence that the practice helped.

Recover useful lessons into their owners. Revisit what no longer fits. Preserve
negative knowledge and actual obligations while allowing mechanisms to change:
understanding carried forward should enable a better next design, not force a
replay of the old one.

Re-finding threads is itself maintenance of understanding. Search across the
corpus by concept, follow plausible relationships, and explain the ones that
matter. Refresh the index and reciprocal links so a later reader can discover
the connection without repeating the whole investigation.

<a id="measure"></a>
## Let The Work Earn Its Machinery

The minimum is enough structure to understand, refer, act, and return. Add
registries, schemas, validators, formal roles, or orchestration when recurring
use exposes a need they can actually meet. Prefer a small truthful practice to
an elaborate account of capabilities that do not exist.

The constitution should grow from observed success, friction, and explicit
direction. New requirements must say which failure they address and who will
maintain their meaning. The shared compact should stay legible while deeper
methods develop with their domains.
